Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

StickerMe is an iOS app that creates personal AI sticker packs from a photo. This policy explains how Vitali Stsiapanau (“StickerMe,” “we,” “us”) handles personal information in connection with the app and these legal pages.

For privacy questions or requests, contact us through the AppInvision contact form and mention StickerMe. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or unnecessary photos.

Information we process

  • Your selected photo and generated images. When you request generation, the app uploads the selected photo to our backend. The photo and the selected pack instructions are sent to our AI provider to create your stickers. We process the resulting images into versions with and without text. Merely browsing packs or selecting a photo for a draft does not submit that photo for generation.
  • App and installation identifiers. We use a generated customer identifier, installation and session identifiers, and Apple-provided app transaction and device-attestation information to verify access and associate purchases, Credits and generation jobs with the correct customer. You do not need to create a separate StickerMe login. These identifiers are not the same as being anonymous to us.
  • Purchase and Credits records. We receive signed purchase information from Apple, such as product and transaction identifiers, subscription dates and status, and purchase, renewal or refund events. We keep records of Credits granted, spent and returned. Apple handles payment details; we do not receive your full payment-card details or Apple Account password.
  • Technical information. Our services and hosting providers may process IP addresses, request timestamps, authentication and security events, error information, generation status, request identifiers and processing usage. This helps operate the service, diagnose failures and prevent misuse.
  • Support communications. If you contact us, we process the information you choose to provide and what is needed to respond.

How we use information

We use this information to generate and deliver the pack you request, manage Credits and subscriptions, restore eligible purchase access, secure sessions, prevent duplicate charges or unauthorized access, resolve technical problems, respond to requests and meet legal obligations.

AI image generation analyzes the appearance in your photo to create stylized images. We do not operate a face-recognition identity service or create a database of face-recognition templates. Apple-based purchase and access verification does not use your submitted face photo.

We do not use your photos or generated stickers to train our own AI models. We do not sell personal information or use your photos for targeted advertising. The current app does not include advertising SDKs or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs.

Service providers and sharing

Information is shared as needed to provide the service:

  • OpenAI: receives the input photo and generation instructions and produces AI images. OpenAI states that API content is not used to train its models by default unless the API customer opts in. Its abuse-monitoring systems may retain content for up to 30 days by default, with exceptions for legal or safety reasons. Our deletion of an image does not itself delete a provider’s separate safety records. See OpenAI’s API data controls.
  • Supabase: provides backend database, authentication, temporary image storage and server functions.
  • Railway: hosts services used for generation processing and verification of Apple-provided proofs and purchase information.
  • Apple: provides App Store purchases, subscriptions, restoration and app/device verification under its own policies. See Apple’s privacy information.
  • GitHub: hosts these legal pages. Visiting them may disclose normal web-request information, including an IP address, to GitHub. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. These pages do not embed analytics, advertising, third-party fonts or tracking scripts.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights or security, or respond to a valid legal request. If you share or export stickers to another app or person, that recipient’s handling of the files is outside our control.

Storage and deletion

  • Source photos and processing files: these are temporary processing data, not a permanent photo library. Our cleanup processes remove them after processing or failure and clear expired uploads. Cleanup is asynchronous and can take longer when a service or network is unavailable.
  • Temporary server results: generated sticker files are available for delivery for up to 72 hours. After the app confirms that a pack has been saved locally, or when the delivery period ends, server cleanup is scheduled. Temporary recovery copies can remain while processing or cleanup completes.
  • Local My Packs: downloaded packs stay on your device until you delete them or the app’s data is removed. StickerMe does not provide a permanent cloud library or cloud backup of your packs. Uninstalling the app or changing devices can permanently remove access to them. Deleting a local pack does not delete copies you previously exported or shared.
  • Operational records: generation-worker diagnostic records are scheduled for cleanup after 30 days. Other security and hosting logs may follow the relevant provider’s retention rules.
  • Identity and financial records: we retain the records needed to maintain Credits, verify purchases, handle restoration and refunds, prevent duplicate processing, resolve disputes and satisfy legal obligations. These records do not follow the short image-delivery retention period. Retention depends on these purposes and applicable requirements.

Backup and provider-held copies may persist beyond deletion from active application storage according to their own retention processes. We do not promise immediate deletion from every backup or third-party system.

Your choices and privacy rights

You can choose not to submit a photo, delete downloaded packs in the app, manage photo access through iOS, and manage subscriptions through Apple. Removing the app does not cancel a subscription or automatically erase server-side purchase records.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on it, and complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us through the link above to exercise these rights. We may need proportionate information to verify that a request concerns your data. Some records may need to be retained for legal, security or financial reasons, and deletion can affect access to Credits or restoration.

Where European or UK data-protection law applies, our purposes generally rely on performing the service you request (generation, delivery and purchase administration), legitimate interests in service security and reliability, and legal obligations. Where consent is required, it must be obtained for the relevant processing and can be withdrawn; withdrawal does not undo processing already lawfully performed. Sticker generation is automated, but we do not use it to make decisions about employment, credit, insurance or similar matters with legal or comparably significant effects.

International processing and security

Our providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States, where data-protection rules may differ. You can contact us for information about the providers and transfer safeguards applicable to your information. Rights and safeguards required by applicable law remain in effect.

We use encrypted network connections, access controls and verification of sensitive requests to protect information. No system can guarantee absolute security. Do not submit photos you are not entitled to share or information that is unnecessary for creating a sticker pack.

Other people and children

If a photo shows another person, you must have the necessary permission to submit and process it, including permission from a parent or legal guardian where required. The app is not a way to collect information about children without appropriate authorization. If you believe a child’s information or someone else’s photo has been submitted unlawfully, contact us so that we can review the request and take appropriate action.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the service or legal requirements change. The date above identifies this version. Where legally required, we will provide additional notice or request consent for a material change.
